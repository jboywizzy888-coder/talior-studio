Clothes that fit the way you imagined them

I am Oluebubechukwu, and Oluchukwu World is my sewing planent. I make dresses, gowns, skirts and native outfits for women, and small, soft, comfortable outfits for baby girls.

I take my own measurements, choose the cut with you, and check every seam before it leaves my hands. If it does not feel right on the body, I fix it.

  • Women only and baby girls only, so every pattern is one I know well
  • Made to measure with a fitting before the final stitch
  • Delivered on the date we agree

What I sew

Women's dresses and gowns

Evening gowns, corporate dresses, bridesmaid sets, aso-ebi and church outfits.

Native and traditional wear

Boubou, wrapper and blouse, iro and buba, and kaftans in Ankara, lace and George.

Baby girls' outfits

Christening gowns, birthday dresses, frocks and matching sets in soft, skin-friendly fabric.

Mother and daughter sets

Matching outfits cut from the same fabric, for photos and family events.

How an order works

  1. Tell me what you want

    Send a picture, a style name, or just describe the occasion.

  2. Measure and choose fabric

    I take your measurements and we agree on fabric, cut and price.

  3. Fitting

    You try the outfit on before the final stitching so adjustments are easy.

  4. Delivery

    Your outfit is pressed, packed and ready on the day we agreed.
